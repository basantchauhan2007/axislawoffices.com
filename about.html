About Axis Law Offices

A law firm built on trust, expertise, and a commitment to results.

Introduction

A Full-Service Law Firm Headquartered in New Delhi

Axis Law Offices is a full-service law firm headquartered in New Delhi, providing legal, advisory and dispute-resolution services across 14 areas of practice. The Firm advises and represents corporates, institutions, entrepreneurs and individuals across a wide range of sectors and legal requirements, with its practice encompassing Arbitration & Dispute Resolution; Banking, Finance & Insurance; Broadcasting, Telecom & Media Laws; Competition Law; Corporate & Commercial; Electricity & Energy Laws; Environment Laws; Intellectual Property Laws; Insolvency & Bankruptcy; Labour & Employment Laws; Civil, Commercial & Criminal Litigation; Mergers & Acquisitions; Property & Real Estate; and Tax Litigation.

The breadth of the Firm's practice enables it to provide clients with integrated legal advice, combining sector-specific understanding with a practical and commercially conscious approach.

Among its areas of particular strength, Arbitration & Dispute Resolution forms a significant part of the Firm's practice, with experience in domestic and international arbitration, mediation and conciliation, enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards, arbitration-related proceedings before the High Courts and the Supreme Court, and the drafting of arbitration agreements and clauses. The Firm represents public sector undertakings, government bodies and private enterprises in complex and high-value disputes.

The Firm also has a strong Intellectual Property Law practice, advising clients across patents, trademarks, copyright, designs and geographical indications. Its work extends from IP prosecution and registration to licensing and assignment, enforcement and infringement actions and representation before Intellectual Property Offices and various Indian Courts. The Firm's IP practice is complemented by its broader corporate, commercial and dispute-resolution capabilities, enabling it to advise clients not only on securing and managing intellectual property rights but also on their commercial exploitation and enforcement.

Another core area of the Firm's practice is Banking, Finance & Insurance, where it advises on banking regulation and compliance, securitisation, project and acquisition finance, derivatives, corporate restructuring, loan and security documentation, proceedings under the SARFAESI Act before the DRT and DRAT, RBI compliance and insurance and reinsurance coverage disputes. This practice enables the Firm to advise financial institutions, corporates and other stakeholders on both transactional and contentious aspects of financial and regulatory matters.

Across its practice areas, Axis Law Offices places emphasis on clear legal advice, responsiveness, attention to detail and practical solutions. The Firm works closely with its clients to understand their objectives and provide advice that is tailored to the particular legal, commercial and strategic considerations of each matter.

Our History

From Chauhan Law Offices to Axis Law Offices

Axis Law Offices was founded by Sagar Chauhan, Advocate, and carries forward the legacy, experience and professional track record of Chauhan Law Offices, which he established and built over more than a decade of legal practice in New Delhi. The transition to Axis Law Offices marks the Firm's continued growth, the expansion of its team and capabilities, and its evolution into a broader, full-service law firm serving clients across diverse areas of law and across India.

The Firm's history, client relationships, courtroom experience and professional values remain at the heart of Axis Law Offices. The work undertaken and relationships developed under the Chauhan Law Offices name continue as an integral part of the Firm's journey. Axis Law Offices builds on this foundation with a wider range of legal capabilities and a growing team of professionals committed to providing practical, responsive and well-considered legal advice and representation.

Today, Axis Law Offices advises and represents clients across 14 areas of practice, including Arbitration & Dispute Resolution; Banking, Finance & Insurance; Broadcasting, Telecom & Media Laws; Competition Law; Corporate & Commercial Law; Electricity & Energy Laws; Environment Laws; Intellectual Property Laws; Insolvency & Bankruptcy; Labour & Employment Laws; Civil, Commercial & Criminal Litigation; Mergers & Acquisitions; Property & Real Estate; and Tax Litigation. This breadth allows the Firm to approach complex matters from multiple legal and commercial perspectives and to provide clients with integrated advice across their evolving requirements.

Among these areas, the Firm has developed particular strength in Arbitration & Dispute Resolution, Intellectual Property Law and Banking, Finance & Insurance. Its dispute-resolution practice encompasses arbitration, commercial disputes and related court proceedings, while its Intellectual Property practice covers the protection, prosecution, management and enforcement of intellectual property rights, including patents, trademarks, copyrights and designs. The Firm's Banking, Finance & Insurance practice further extends its expertise to financial and regulatory matters, including banking transactions, finance, securitisation, recovery proceedings and insurance-related disputes.

The Firm is led by Founding & Managing Partner Sagar Chauhan, alongside Senior Partner Rohit Kathuria and Partners Sanjay Kumar Yadav and Dhruv Varma, supported by a dedicated team of Associates and legal professionals whose collective experience spans civil, commercial, criminal, constitutional, regulatory and specialised areas of legal practice. Together, the leadership team brings a broad range of experience to the Firm while retaining the personal attention, accessibility and commitment that have shaped its practice from the outset.
