Practice Areas

Comprehensive legal services across 11 areas of law — from arbitration and commercial litigation to corporate transactions and regulatory compliance.

Core Specialisations

Areas of Particular Depth

These four areas represent the deepest specialisations of our Founding Partner and senior counsel.

⚖

Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

Our practice covers the full spectrum of domestic and international arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, the ICA Rules, and UNCITRAL/ICC frameworks. We handle mediation, conciliation, enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards, and representation in arbitration petitions before the High Courts and the Supreme Court. Our team has extensive experience drafting arbitration clauses and representing PSUs and corporates — including Power Grid Corporation, NHAI, and the Government of NCT — in high-value proceedings.

⚖

Civil, Commercial & White-Collar Litigation

We provide end-to-end representation across the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Delhi, and all District Courts, as well as specialised tribunals including NCLT, DRT, DRAT, TDSAT, NGT, RERA, and the Central Administrative Tribunal. Our litigation practice covers commercial disputes, civil suits, writ petitions, and white-collar crime matters — including economic offences, corporate fraud, and cheque dishonour proceedings.

⚖

Intellectual Property Law

Our Intellectual Property practice covers the protection, prosecution, licensing and enforcement of patents, trade marks, copyright, designs and geographical indications under Indian law. We handle clearance, filing and prosecution, oppositions, rectification and revocation, together with infringement and passing-off actions before the statutory authorities and the courts. We also advise on assignments, technology transfer, licensing and franchising, and on building and commercialising IP portfolios, including matters with cross-border dimensions.

⚖

Insolvency & Bankruptcy

We advise creditors, debtors, resolution professionals, and other stakeholders under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. Our work spans distressed asset restructuring, debt resolution strategies, corporate insolvency resolution processes (CIRP), and representation before the NCLT and NCLAT. We provide practical guidance to both financial creditors and operational creditors navigating India's insolvency framework.

01Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

+

The Arbitration and Dispute Resolution practice encompasses domestic and international arbitration, mediation, conciliation and other forms of alternative dispute resolution. The practice covers arbitration proceedings under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, as well as proceedings conducted under institutional and international frameworks, including ICC, UNCITRAL and ICA Rules. It includes the drafting, review and enforcement of arbitration agreements and dispute resolution clauses, together with advice on the conduct and strategy of arbitral proceedings. The practice further extends to applications for interim measures, appointment and constitution of arbitral tribunals, challenges to arbitral awards and proceedings for enforcement of awards. Representation is undertaken before arbitral tribunals, the High Courts and the Supreme Court of India in arbitration-related matters. The Firm has acted for public sector undertakings, government bodies and private enterprises in substantial and complex commercial disputes. Particular emphasis is placed on the formulation of effective dispute resolution strategies having regard to the legal, commercial and jurisdictional dimensions of each matter. The practice is directed towards achieving efficient, legally sustainable and commercially appropriate resolution of disputes.

02Civil, Commercial & White-Collar Litigation

+

The Civil, Commercial and White-Collar Litigation practice encompasses complex civil, commercial, constitutional and criminal proceedings before courts and specialised tribunals. The practice covers commercial disputes, contractual claims, civil suits, writ proceedings, injunctions, recovery actions and proceedings arising from corporate and business relationships. It further extends to white-collar crime, economic offences, corporate fraud, financial irregularities, criminal breach of trust, cheating and proceedings arising from dishonour of cheques under applicable law. Representation is undertaken before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts, District Courts and specialised forums including the NCLT, DRT, DRAT, NGT, TDSAT, RERA and CAT. The practice includes both contentious representation and pre-litigation advisory, with emphasis on identifying legal and evidentiary issues at an early stage. Advice is provided on litigation strategy, interim relief, settlement, enforcement and appellate remedies. The Firm also acts in matters involving overlapping civil, commercial, regulatory and criminal proceedings. The practice is founded upon rigorous legal analysis, effective advocacy and a clear appreciation of the commercial and reputational interests involved.

03Intellectual Property Law

+

The Intellectual Property practice encompasses the protection, prosecution, commercialisation, licensing and enforcement of intellectual property rights under Indian law. The practice covers patents under the Patents Act, 1970, including patent strategy, drafting and prosecution, examination, oppositions, revocation, compulsory licensing and infringement proceedings. It further covers trade marks under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, including clearance, filing, prosecution, opposition, rectification, assignment, licensing, infringement and passing-off actions. The practice includes copyright protection under the Copyright Act, 1957, covering literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works, cinematograph films, sound recordings, software and other protected works, together with registration, licensing and enforcement. Advice is also provided in relation to industrial designs under the Designs Act, 2000, geographical indications under the Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999, and the protection and enforcement of rights relating to such commercial identifiers and creative works. The Firm advises on assignments, technology transfer, licensing, franchising, co-existence arrangements, confidentiality and commercialisation of intellectual property assets. The practice also covers infringement, passing off, opposition, cancellation, rectification, invalidity and other contentious proceedings before the relevant statutory authorities and courts. The Firm provides integrated advice on building, protecting, enforcing and commercially exploiting intellectual property portfolios in India, including matters having international and cross-border dimensions.

04Insolvency & Bankruptcy

+

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy practice encompasses advisory and representation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 and related laws governing corporate and individual insolvency. The practice covers insolvency initiation proceedings, claims, debt restructuring, distressed assets, corporate insolvency resolution processes and liquidation proceedings. It includes representation of financial creditors, operational creditors, corporate debtors, resolution professionals and other stakeholders before the NCLT and NCLAT. The practice further extends to avoidance transactions, preferential and fraudulent transactions, resolution plans and issues arising during the insolvency resolution process. Advice is provided on recovery strategies, restructuring options and the legal consequences of financial distress. The Firm also advises on matters involving the interaction of insolvency proceedings with contractual, security, corporate and regulatory rights. Representation is undertaken in contentious proceedings arising from insolvency and restructuring transactions. The practice adopts a commercially informed approach directed towards preservation and maximisation of value while protecting the legal interests of the client.

05Corporate, Commercial, Mergers & Acquisitions

+

The Corporate and Commercial practice encompasses corporate structuring, incorporation, joint ventures, foreign investment, commercial contracts, corporate restructuring and general corporate advisory. The practice covers mergers and acquisitions, including transaction structuring, legal and corporate due diligence, negotiation and preparation of transaction documentation and regulatory approvals. It includes advice relating to Share Purchase Agreements, Shareholders’ Agreements, Joint Venture Agreements and other investment and corporate arrangements. The practice further extends to FDI, SEBI and other regulatory requirements applicable to corporate transactions. Advice is provided throughout the business lifecycle, from establishment and investment to restructuring and exit. The Firm also assists clients in identifying and managing contractual, regulatory and corporate risks arising from commercial operations. The practice is designed to provide legally sound and commercially practical advice in connection with domestic and cross-border transactions.

06Banking, Finance, Insurance & Recovery Laws

+

The Banking, Finance and Insurance practice encompasses regulatory, transactional and contentious matters arising from banking and financial activities. The practice covers loan and security documentation, project finance, acquisition finance, securitisation, restructuring and related financial transactions. It includes advice on regulatory requirements of the Reserve Bank of India and proceedings under the SARFAESI Act and allied recovery laws. Representation is undertaken before the DRT, DRAT, High Courts and other appropriate forums in recovery and banking disputes. The practice further extends to insurance and reinsurance matters, including policy interpretation, coverage disputes and claims. Advice is provided to lenders, borrowers, financial institutions, insurers and corporate clients on legal and regulatory aspects of financial transactions. The practice combines transactional understanding with experience in contentious proceedings involving financial and security interests.

07Energy, Infrastructure, Environment & Regulatory Laws

+

The Energy, Infrastructure and Environment practice encompasses legal and regulatory matters arising from power, renewable energy, infrastructure and environmentally regulated projects. The practice covers project documentation, Power Purchase Agreements, EPC and O&M contracts, concession arrangements and regulatory approvals. It includes representation before the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission, State Electricity Regulatory Commissions and the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity. The practice further extends to environmental compliance under applicable environmental legislation and proceedings before the National Green Tribunal, High Courts and the Supreme Court of India. Advice is provided on regulatory permissions, contractual obligations, project implementation and disputes arising from energy and infrastructure projects. The Firm also advises clients on the legal implications of environmental regulation and compliance requirements. The practice is directed towards addressing the contractual, regulatory and litigation issues that arise throughout the life of major projects.

08Media, Telecom, Technology & Cyber Law

+

The Media, Telecom, Technology and Cyber Law practice encompasses legal and regulatory issues arising from technology-driven businesses and highly regulated communication sectors. The practice covers broadcasting, telecommunications, digital platforms, Internet services, media operations and related licensing and regulatory requirements. It includes representation before TDSAT, High Courts and the Supreme Court of India in regulatory and licensing disputes. The practice further extends to cyber fraud, data breaches, electronic evidence, online defamation, digital contracts and offences under applicable cyber laws. Advice is provided on technology-related agreements, information technology compliance, data protection and management of legal risks arising from digital operations. The Firm also represents clients in cyber-crime proceedings and disputes involving electronic records and digital transactions. The practice combines regulatory understanding with litigation capability in matters where technology and law intersect.

09Labour & Employment Law

+

The Labour and Employment practice encompasses advisory and contentious matters arising from the employment relationship and applicable labour legislation. The practice covers employment contracts, service rules, HR policies, disciplinary proceedings, termination, retrenchment and statutory compliance. It includes advice on industrial disputes, employment-related claims and workplace obligations. Representation is undertaken before Labour Courts, Industrial Tribunals, High Courts and other appropriate forums. The practice further extends to disputes concerning employment conditions, service matters and employer-employee relationships. The Firm advises employers on preventive legal measures designed to minimise employment-related disputes and regulatory exposure. The practice also provides representation in contentious matters where negotiation, settlement or litigation becomes necessary.

10Real Estate, Property & RERA Laws

+

The Real Estate and Property practice encompasses transactional, regulatory and contentious matters relating to immovable property and real estate development. The practice covers title due diligence, land acquisition, development arrangements, joint development agreements, sale deeds, lease deeds and related property documentation. It includes advice concerning township, infrastructure and real estate projects from acquisition through development and completion. The practice further extends to representation before RERA authorities and courts in matters involving developers, promoters, investors and homebuyers. The Firm advises on title, possession, contractual obligations, development rights and regulatory compliance. Representation is also undertaken in property disputes and proceedings arising from real estate transactions. The practice is directed towards identifying title and regulatory risks at an early stage and providing clear legal solutions to complex property matters.

11Competition & Tax Litigation

+

The Competition and Tax practice encompasses regulatory and contentious matters arising under competition, direct tax and indirect tax laws. The competition practice covers cartel investigations, abuse of dominant position, merger control, competition compliance and proceedings before the Competition Commission of India and appropriate appellate and judicial forums. The tax practice includes advisory and litigation relating to GST, customs, central excise, service tax, VAT and other applicable indirect tax regimes. It further extends to assessments, appeals, regulatory proceedings and disputes before CESTAT, ITAT, High Courts and the Supreme Court of India. The Firm advises businesses on statutory compliance, regulatory exposure and strategies for managing contentious tax and competition matters. Representation is undertaken in matters requiring detailed interpretation of statutory provisions, delegated legislation and regulatory orders. The practice combines advisory capability with strong contentious representation in complex regulatory disputes.
