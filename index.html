Who We Are

One Firm. Complete Legal Solutions.

Axis Law Offices is a full-service law firm headquartered in New Delhi, providing legal, advisory and dispute-resolution services across 14 areas of practice.

The Firm advises and represents corporates, institutions, entrepreneurs and individuals across a wide range of sectors and legal requirements, with its practice encompassing Arbitration & Dispute Resolution; Banking, Finance & Insurance; Intellectual Property Laws; Broadcasting, Telecom & Media Laws; Competition Law; Corporate & Commercial; Electricity & Energy Laws; Environment Laws; Insolvency & Bankruptcy; Labour & Employment Laws; Civil, Commercial & Criminal Litigation; Mergers & Acquisitions; Property & Real Estate; and Tax Litigation.

The breadth of the Firm's practice enables it to provide clients with integrated legal advice, combining sector-specific understanding with a practical and commercially conscious approach.
