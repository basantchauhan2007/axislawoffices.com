Sanjay Kumar Yadav

Sanjay Kumar Yadav is a Partner at Axis Law Offices with over ten years of experience in legal practice. His practice spans Banking and Financial Laws, Intellectual Property Rights, particularly trademarks and copyrights, civil and commercial litigation, consumer laws, service laws, labour laws, and criminal matters. He advises and represents clients across a range of contentious and advisory matters, with experience before courts, tribunals and other judicial and quasi-judicial forums.

Sanjay has developed a strong practice in banking and financial matters, advising and representing clients in disputes and proceedings involving banking transactions, financial claims, recovery proceedings, contractual obligations and related regulatory issues. His understanding of banking and commercial laws enables him to assist clients in navigating complex financial and legal disputes while adopting a practical and solution-oriented approach.

His Intellectual Property practice focuses on trademarks and copyrights, covering matters relating to the protection, enforcement and commercial use of intellectual property rights. He advises clients on trademark and copyright disputes, infringement, passing off, enforcement and other related issues. His practice also includes assisting businesses with the legal aspects of protecting and managing their brands, creative works and other intellectual assets.

Sanjay has extensive experience in civil and commercial litigation and regularly handles matters involving contractual disputes, property-related issues, consumer claims and other civil proceedings. He also advises and represents clients in criminal matters, bringing a thorough understanding of procedural and substantive law to complex and sensitive cases.

His practice further encompasses service and labour laws, including disputes arising from employment relationships, service conditions, workplace matters and related legal proceedings. He has represented clients before various forums in and around Delhi and has experience handling matters requiring careful consideration of both legal and factual issues.

Sanjay's diverse litigation experience enables him to approach matters from a comprehensive perspective, identifying legal risks and developing practical strategies for effective resolution. His ability to work across banking, intellectual property, civil, consumer, criminal, service and labour matters allows him to provide clients with integrated legal support across varied areas of practice.

Known for his diligent and detail-oriented approach, Sanjay focuses on understanding the specific circumstances and objectives of each matter before developing a legal strategy. He is committed to providing clear, practical and effective advice while ensuring that clients receive responsive representation throughout the course of their legal proceedings.
