Dhruv Varma

Dhruv Varma is a Partner at Axis Law Offices and has been practising law since 2018. A graduate of Amity Law School, Noida, his practice encompasses Corporate Law, Intellectual Property Rights, particularly patents and industrial designs, Mergers & Acquisitions, commercial litigation and Alternative Dispute Resolution. He advises clients across a broad range of corporate and commercial matters and represents them in complex disputes before various courts, tribunals and arbitral forums.

Dhruv's corporate practice includes advising on corporate and commercial matters, contractual arrangements, corporate structuring, regulatory issues and strategic transactions. He has particular experience in Mergers & Acquisitions, including legal due diligence, transaction structuring, review and negotiation of transaction documentation, assessment of legal and regulatory risks, and other matters arising throughout the transaction lifecycle.

His Intellectual Property practice focuses on patents and industrial designs, including matters relating to the protection, enforcement, commercialisation and strategic management of intellectual property rights. He advises clients on IP-related transactions and agreements and assists businesses in addressing intellectual property considerations within their broader commercial and corporate objectives.

Alongside his transactional practice, Dhruv has extensive experience in commercial litigation and arbitration before various judicial, quasi-judicial and arbitral forums. His primary focus in dispute resolution includes commercial, banking, electricity and consumer matters. He has handled disputes across diverse industry sectors involving arbitration law, contract law and company law, providing him with a strong understanding of the procedural, commercial and legal issues arising in complex business disputes.

Dhruv has also conducted legal due diligence for banks and companies and has advised on commercial contracts and arbitration agreements. His experience across both transactional and contentious matters enables him to identify potential legal and commercial risks at an early stage and develop practical strategies for addressing them.

His practice involves matters across diverse industry sectors, requiring an understanding of the regulatory and commercial frameworks applicable to businesses and their operations. Dhruv combines his experience in corporate transactions and intellectual property with his background in litigation and dispute resolution, allowing him to approach legal issues from both transactional and contentious perspectives.

Dhruv brings a commercially focused, analytical and detail-oriented approach to his practice. He is committed to providing clear, practical and strategic legal advice, with a focus on understanding each client's objectives and delivering solutions that are aligned with their broader commercial interests.
